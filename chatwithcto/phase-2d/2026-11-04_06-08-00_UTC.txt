## 3. Investigation & Resolution of Update Accounting Defect

### 3.1 Defect Root Cause Analysis
In Phase 2D.7A, an update with 2 additions and 1 removal displayed:
```
New records added: 1
Removed records: 0
```
**Root Cause:**
In `crates/raxosentinel-signatures/src/rsidb/updater/engine.rs`:
```rust
// BUGGY IMPLEMENTATION in Phase 2D.7A:
let new_records = final_count.saturating_sub(previous_total_count);
let removed_records = previous_total_count.saturating_sub(final_count);
```
This conflated **net growth** with **gross additions**, and completely masked removals whenever net growth was positive ($542,058 - 542,057 = 1$, and $542,057 \ominus 542,058 = 0$).

### 3.2 Implemented Set-Theoretic Accounting
The update engine now enforces strict set-theoretic classification of every indicator key:
- **Gross new unique records (Additions)**:
  $$additions = |K_{final} \setminus K_{initial}|$$
- **Records removed or revoked (Removals)**:
  $$removals = |K_{initial} \setminus K_{final}|$$
- **Records retained through another provider**:
  Keys in $K_{initial} \cap K_{final}$ that were previously claimed by an updating source, but now have no claims from any updating source (supported solely by non-updating sources).
- **Net count change**:
  $$\Delta = final\_count - initial\_count = additions - removals$$
- **Strict Invariant Enforcement**:
  $$final\_count \equiv initial\_count + additions - removals$$
  If this invariant is violated by even 1 indicator, the update engine aborts and **fails closed**.
